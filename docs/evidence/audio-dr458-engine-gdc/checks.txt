- Each loop's 4th-order line measured after cutting: within 2 % of its rpm (worst: off 1,500 at 1,470).
- Seams after Vorbis: at most 0.57 of the loop's 99.9th-percentile sample step (idle 0.568, the rest
  0.013-0.42). The packed bank peaks under -1 dBFS (asserted by the tool).
- bank.ogg 108.333 s, events.ogg 14.692 s, mono 48 kHz Vorbis q6. Uploaded by the coordinator:
  bank 134452098270493, events 79771227703971.
- Re-baking with the off-volume floor reproduces the uploaded audio: the pre-encode WAVs are
  byte-identical and the decoded OGGs identical sample for sample (the OGG bytes differ only by the
  Ogg stream serial number).
- Regions are written in microseconds, each start rounded up and each end rounded down, so a loop
  can lose a sample but never play the silence after it.
