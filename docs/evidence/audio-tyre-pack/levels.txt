file read as two channels (mono doubled), as the engine and road packs were matched.

3. The engine's level: the loudness curve against what the layers play (sqrt of the sum of
   EngineSound.step's mix.volumes squared), on the preview timeline:
     SVJ:   within 0.1 dB in a held drift; -0.7 to +1.9 dB in the lock's first 0.5 s (smoothing)
     DR458: -0.1 dB in a held drift; -4.1 to -7.0 dB once the throttle closes (its off-load
            layers carry volume 0.47 to 0.67)
   So the ceiling reads the played level, not the curve.

4. Ceiling, as agreed for Rules/TyreAudio:
     sqrt(sum over both axles of (squeal_v^2 + skid_v^2)) <= engineLevel x 10^((offsetDb - headroomDb)/20)
     engineLevel = pack.engineVolume x sqrt(sum mix.volumes^2), no shift dip, no start-up share
     offsetDb -1.9, headroomDb 3; base volumes squeal 0.45, skid 0.45
