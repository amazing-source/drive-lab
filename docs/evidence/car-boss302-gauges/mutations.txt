== Per-frame cost
The rule work Controllers/Gauges does each frame for the Boss (engine step, oil pressure, six dials'
share and needle step), Lune: median 0.99 us, best 0.91, worst 1.06 (7 runs of 100,000 frames).
The controller's engine side (attribute reads, six Weld.C0 writes, twelve EmissiveStrength checks) is
not measured: it needs Studio, and the Boss is not in the place yet.
