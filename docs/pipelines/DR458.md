# DR458 pipeline: measured checks for the second car

The DR458 (an unbadged Ferrari 458 Challenge Evo) is assembled in Blender from a road-car body,
third-party race parts and our own cockpit pieces. Its working files stay outside the repo
(`Downloads/drive-lab-assets/dr458/work/`, set `DR458_WORK` to it); the repo holds the scripts.
The owner's bar is that every accuracy claim is measured, so each step below has a check that was
shown failing once before it was trusted. Usage is in each script's header.

## Mesh hygiene

| Script | What it does | Shown failing |
|---|---|---|
| `tools/blender/dr458/dump_mesh.py` | Every visible mesh in world space to one `.npz`, for numpy analysis without Blender | - |
| `tools/blender/dr458/zfight_scan.py` | Pairs of meshes within 1.5 mm facing the same way: they flicker on single-sided MeshParts. Back-to-back faces are counted apart (one is always culled) | On the master as received: 323 pairs over 1 cm2 |
| `tools/blender/dr458/clean_hidden.py` | Deletes what the race car lacks (badges, struts, road door cards), cuts faces no viewpoint sees from their front side, treats depth-fight pairs (delete the covered under-faces, lift the overlay 2.5 mm) | - |
| `tools/blender/dr458/render_ids.py` + `tools/assets/dr458/render_diff.py` | Seeded views, one flat colour per object, before against after; changed pixels are attributed to the object that was there, excusing deliberate deletions and resolved pairs | The first clean-up cut the visible front-intake backing: 13,815 px, 4.2 % of a view, named by the diff |
| `tools/blender/dr458/budget_decimate.py` | Per-object collapse decimation, binary search on a surface-deviation tolerance, to the plan's triangle lines | not run yet |

Two traps found on the way:
- A clean-up that judges visibility by face centres alone deletes faces seen only at their
  corners; the script also casts at points near each corner and keeps the neighbours of every kept
  face.
- Workbench renders with the AgX view transform by default, which changes flat colours; the ID
  renders set Standard, and the diff matches colours to the nearest palette entry within 4 levels
  (Workbench dithers by one level).

## Photo-matched cameras

`overlay/v2/` in the work folder (moving into the repo when its solves pass): landmarks computed
from the master's geometry, a pinhole solver with a verdict (at least 7 points, focal to 5 %, every
left-out landmark within 40 mm, median within 15 mm), and a self-test proving it fails on a click
80 mm off. As of this writing the real-photo solves of exterior 001 fail that verdict.

## Manifest

`tools/assets/dr458/merge_manifest.py` merges the per-area manifests (exterior by the 3D lead,
cockpit hardware, cockpit pieces) into `dr458_manifest.json` for the systems agent and refuses
the merge on any problem: missing fields, non-unit axes, ranges out of kind, a moving part without
an agreed contract name, a material missing from the library, a piece over 10,000 triangles.
Shown failing on three deliberate breaks (non-unit axis, renamed key, unknown material).

Rotation sense: axes are Blender axes converted to `{forward = -y, right = -x, up = z}`. That frame
is left-handed, but mapped to Roblox as (x = right, y = up, z = -forward) the chain is Blender
(x, y, z) -> Roblox (-x, z, y), a proper rotation, so a positive turn keeps its sense.

## Edit-mode preview without upload

`tools/blender/dr458/piece_json.py` writes pieces as triangle lists in Roblox axes; a Studio script
builds them as EditableMeshes and MeshParts from a local server, beside the SVJ, and removes them
afterwards. Not yet run in Studio.
