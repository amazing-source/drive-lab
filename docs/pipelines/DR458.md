# DR458 pipeline: the second car, from road-car body to Roblox-ready master

The DR458 (an unbadged Ferrari 458 Challenge Evo) is assembled in Blender from a road-car body,
third-party race parts and our own cockpit pieces. Its working files stay outside the repo
(`Downloads/drive-lab-assets/dr458/work/`, set `DR458_WORK` to it); the repo holds the scripts.
The bar is an excellent driver view and a faithful car on the main things, so work is ranked by
what the driver sees from the seat, then by the chase camera. Every check below was shown failing
once before it was trusted. Usage is in each script's header; every Blender job runs alone under
the work folder's lock script.

## Order

| Step | Script | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `tools/blender/dr458/clean_hidden.py` | From the assembled master: deletes what the race car lacks (badges, struts, road door cards), ends the cabin's inner skin at the rear window, and cuts faces no game viewpoint sees |
| 2 | `tools/blender/dr458/cuts.py` + `cuts_dr458.json` | Removes road-car interior the race car does not have, by boxes (console, tach housing, road pedals, rear-wall top band); moves the driver's door glass into the door group |
| 3 | `tools/blender/dr458/budget_decimate.py` | Decimates each object to the tightest surface tolerance that still fits each area's triangle line; the cabin's inner skin is welded and keeps its outline |
| 4 | `tools/blender/dr458/zfix.py` | Separates surfaces that would depth-fight; runs after decimation, which moves surfaces by millimetres. Never moves the inner skin, never deletes what lies behind glass |
| 4b | `tools/blender/dr458/backing.py` | `find` on the assembled car, `apply` on the master: a reversed copy of every master face the cockpit eye sees from behind, in its own object |
| 4c | `tools/blender/dr458/retire_objects.py` | Moves master objects another file replaces (the road dash, replaced by the cockpit files' race dash) into the hidden `Replaced_Cabin`, kept in the file and never exported |
| 4d | `tools/blender/dr458/exhaust_cut.py` | Cuts the rear bumper 2 mm outside each exhaust barrel where the barrel passes through it: an exact boolean with a cutter turned from the barrel's own radius profile. Nothing moves |
| 5 | `tools/blender/dr458/inventory.py`, `tools/blender/dr458/windscreen_points.py`, `tools/assets/dr458/exterior_manifest.py`, `tools/assets/dr458/merge_manifest.py` | The master's parts list; the windscreen's four corners as manifest points; the exterior manifest (wheels, door, glass, exhaust outlets, and the lamps the source separates, under the game's `Light_<Corner>_<Role>` names); the merged, validated manifest for the systems binding |

The materials and livery (the materials pipeline's apply, livery bake, apply) run before steps 4c-4d
and the backing, on the file before the livery: the bake finds the paint by its pre-livery material,
so on its own output it finds almost no faces.

The visibility cut casts single-sided rays, as Roblox draws MeshParts: a face met from behind
neither counts as seen nor blocks the ray. Its viewpoints come from the game's own limits: the
cockpit eye over `Config/CarCamera.EYE_RANGE`, on-foot rings 0.5 and 1.5 m outside the body, far
rings, and the open driver's door (the only door that opens).

## Traps found on the way

- Roblox draws one side. A check rendered with both sides passes a model with holes, and a
  visibility test that lets back faces occlude cuts faces the player sees.
- A road-car export is split at every hard edge and UV seam: an "open boundary" there is not the
  object's edge, so boundary-aware decimation freezes the mesh.
- Decimating to the lowest ratio a tolerance allows wastes the budget; take the tightest
  tolerance that fits the line instead.
- Depth-fight fixes go after decimation. Move the overlay (a strip, a rim, a decal), never the
  large surface under it: its vertices are nowhere near the strip.
- Appending a parts file: link its parent empties too, or children land at the world origin.
- Workbench renders with AgX by default, which changes flat colours; ID renders set Standard.
- In Blender's Decimate modifier a vertex-group weight of 0 protects a vertex and 1 leaves it free.
  The reverse froze the inner skin at every ratio and let its outline retreat 210 mm; a deviation
  tolerance sampled over the area does not see an outline retreating along a thin strip.
- A filler surface sits a few millimetres inside a shell: moving it to stop a depth fight pulls its
  outline away from what it meets and opens slits to the sky. Delete its covered faces, or move the
  other surface.
- An exact boolean with hole tolerance on an open surface keeps the cutter's own faces wherever they
  fall inside the surface's winding: the exhaust cut kept 1,126 faces of its cutters (a sleeve 2 mm
  round each barrel and two caps). Delete the faces lying wholly on a cutter, then verify.
- A loop variable that shadows a function parameter can hold an object a `join()` has freed; writing
  it into an ID property crashes Blender (exit 139, `BPy_IDProperty_FromPyObject` in the crash log).
- A job chain that runs on after a failure builds on half-done files (a failed bake still saved its
  blend and its maps): stop at the first failing job. Piping Blender into `head` can hide the
  summary under the exporter's INFO lines: filter them, or read the report file.
- Glass is transparent: never delete what lies behind it, and never move a glass layer that sits
  between two others (a frit band bounced between the clear glass and the surround).
- A new-holes check hides what was already wrong. Render both sides: wherever the both-sided frame
  differs from the single-sided one, the eye looks through a surface from behind.

## Export (dry run)

`tools/blender/dr458/export_dr458.py` on the assembled car: manifest parts keep their names (a part
with several materials splits into `<Name>_<Material>`), other geometry merges per area and
material into `<Area>_<Material>_<nn>`, each MeshPart at most 10,000 triangles, every piece tagged
`Part` and `Group` for the binding; studs at scale 1 in the manifest's frame; lit faces keep their
rotation with +Z toward the driver. It reads the glb back and fails on a missing part, an oversize
piece or a primitive without tangents. Nothing is uploaded.

## Edit-mode preview without upload

`tools/blender/dr458/piece_json.py` writes pieces as triangle lists in Roblox axes; a Studio
script builds them as EditableMeshes and MeshParts from a local server and removes them afterwards.
